Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Purpose

This personal setup uses rclone to transfer selected files from my Pulsed Media seedbox to my own Google Drive account.

Google Drive data

With my authorization, rclone accesses the Google Drive files permitted by the OAuth permissions I grant. File contents, names, and relevant metadata are processed to transfer and verify uploads.

Storage and sharing

Uploaded files are stored in my Google Drive account. The rclone configuration and authorization tokens are stored in my private seedbox account for the transfer to work. Transfer logs may contain file names and transfer status.

I do not intentionally sell or share the transferred files with other people. Google processes files and API requests according to its own service terms and privacy policies.

Retention and access revocation

Files remain in Google Drive until I delete them. I can revoke the application's Google account access through my Google Account security settings. Local files and transfer logs are managed separately on the seedbox.

Security

Authorization credentials are intended to remain private and are not published on this website. No file contents are displayed or collected by this informational website itself.

Contact

Questions about this setup can be directed through the contact section of my main website.
